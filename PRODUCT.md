# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Current prototype: Next.js with TypeScript and Vercel-ready static deployment. Future production version can add Supabase, private image storage, and email notifications.

## Users

Residents report weak drainage points. Volunteers and cleanup coordinators use the action plan to prepare before rain. A coordinator reviews proof before the readiness score changes.

## Product Purpose

DrainSense turns a rain warning plus local weak-point reports into a ranked cleanup route, allowing a local group to act before rain and record reviewed before-and-after evidence. Success is a credible, working rain-warning-to-proof flow for the Earth Forward hackathon.

## Positioning

DrainSense is a community action system rather than a passive environmental dashboard: it connects rain readiness, weak-point priority, cleanup coordination, resolution evidence, and truthful impact.

## Operating Context

People use the mobile-first web app before a rain window or while organizing community work. The website is the task-status source of truth; manual invite copy and calendar links support coordination without exposing personal phone numbers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

English-first, light-only, truthful sample-data prototype at launch. The product must not claim official emergency alerts, municipal or NGO partnerships, live field measurements, real flood prediction, or automated WhatsApp/SMS delivery unless those are actually configured.

## Brand Commitments

Name: DrainSense. Tagline: Find weak points before rain. Prove the cleanup after. Voice: calm, practical, strict, and safety-first. The interface is light-only.

## Evidence on Hand

No real partner organization, field reports, or measured environmental outcomes have been supplied. Demo fixtures must be clearly labelled as sample or demo data.

## Product Principles

- Turn pre-rain warnings into accountable community action.
- Make safety, privacy, and truthful impact visible in the workflow.
- Keep field actions fast on mobile and understandable for first-time users.
- Preserve a complete, demonstrable flow before adding optional integrations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard access, visible focus, text labels in addition to status color, accessible form feedback, and a list alternative for map content.
