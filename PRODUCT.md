# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js with TypeScript, Supabase, and Vercel. This was selected in the approved implementation plan because it supports secure email accounts, image storage, a community workflow, and Vercel deployment.

## Users

Residents report blocked drains. Volunteers join safe cleanup events. Cleanup coordinators run community groups such as NGOs, school eco-clubs, and resident associations. Moderators review reports and organizations.

## Product Purpose

DrainSense turns a resident photo and location into a reviewed cleanup task, allowing a local group to organize safe action and record verified before-and-after evidence. Success is a credible, working report-to-resolution flow for the Earth Forward hackathon.

## Positioning

DrainSense is a community action system rather than a passive environmental dashboard: it connects a report to verification, cleanup coordination, resolution evidence, and truthful impact.

## Operating Context

People use the mobile-first web app outdoors or while organizing community work. The website is the task-status source of truth; in-app notifications and email deliver updates. WhatsApp sharing and calendar links support coordination without exposing personal phone numbers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

English-first, email-account access for active features, public anonymized map, and a truthful sample-data prototype at launch. The product must not claim municipal or NGO partnerships, live field measurements, or automated WhatsApp/SMS delivery unless those are actually configured. It must not use a dark UI.

## Brand Commitments

Name: DrainSense. Tagline: Turn one photo into flood prevention. Voice: calm, practical, hopeful, and safety-first. The interface is light-only.

## Evidence on Hand

No real partner organization, field reports, or measured environmental outcomes have been supplied. Demo fixtures must be clearly labelled as sample or demo data.

## Product Principles

- Turn reporting into accountable community action.
- Make safety, privacy, and truthful impact visible in the workflow.
- Keep field actions fast on mobile and understandable for first-time users.
- Preserve a complete, demonstrable flow before adding optional integrations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard access, visible focus, text labels in addition to status color, accessible form feedback, and a list alternative for map content.
